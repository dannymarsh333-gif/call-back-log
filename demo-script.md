# Demo Script (2 minutes, screen recording, NOT a pitch)

Show the app working with one fake contact, cold call to follow-up.
1. Home: today's callbacks, one yellow and one red overdue.
2. Open a contact card.
3. Finish a call: guided voice debrief (topics, companies, next steps).
4. Callback date set; contact appears on the right day.
5. Next day: follow-up call, card shows everything from last time.
