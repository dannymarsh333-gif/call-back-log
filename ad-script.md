# Ad script

## Length
60 seconds.

## The scenes
| Seconds | On screen | Voice or text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | THE HOOK. A stick figure sits at a desk in a plain office, phone to his ear, about to hang up. | Stick figure (cheerful, rushed): "Ok, alright, sounds good, FANTASTIC, great, have a great day!" | Office hum. The phone clicks as he hangs up. |
| 6–12 | He puts the phone down and goes still. A thought bubble appears over his head. | Thought bubble text: "What did I just talk about for the past hour?" | The cheerful sound drops out. Silence, then a clock ticking. |
| 12–20 | THE PROBLEM MOMENT. A coworker (another stick figure) walks up and drops a tall stack of papers on his desk. | Coworker: "Here, some notes from my calls today. Organize this." | A loud thud, papers rustling, the coworker's footsteps leaving. |
| 20–26 | The stick figure stares at the stack. His eyes spiral and thought bubbles fill with scribbles: sticky notes, phone numbers, question marks, clocks. He is overstimulated. | Thought bubble text: "How could I make this system better?" | Rapid clicking and buzzing that builds, then cuts to a single "ding." |
| 26–34 | THE APP SOLVING IT. Cut to a real screen recording of Call Back Log on a phone (sample data only). The Today's callbacks list appears, then the "Finish call" questions. | Narrator (calm, friendly): "Meet Call Back Log. After each call, say what happened: topics, companies, next steps, even when to call back. It keeps it all in one place and tells you who to call today." | Music comes in: light and upbeat. Soft taps as each screen changes. |
| 34–48 | The contact card for Jane Doe (made-up). The screen scrolls down her timeline, newest call first. The "Open issues" box shows at the top. Highlight each call as the narrator mentions it. | Narrator: "Here's Jane Doe. Her newest call: she's comparing term and whole life and already has coverage with Acme Mutual. Last week she mentioned a baby due in March and worried about cost. Before that, she asked about disability coverage. Every conversation, in order." | Music continues. A gentle "whoosh" as the screen scrolls. |
| 48–55 | The view pans back out to the stick figure at his desk. He points at the stack of papers. The papers vanish in a puff of cartoon smoke. The app screen appears beside him showing the new entries, all organized. | Stick figure (relieved): "Oh. That's better." | A "poof" sound, then a soft happy chime. |
| 55–60 | Title card on a calm blue background with the app name. | Narrator: "Call Back Log. Remember every call." | Music ends with a short button. Silence. |

## Jane Doe (made-up sample contact for the scroll)
All made up. Add her to the app's sample data before filming.
- **Newest call:** comparing term and whole life. Already covered by Acme Mutual. Worried about cost.
- **A week earlier:** mentioned a baby due in March.
- **Before that:** asked about disability coverage and mentioned Harbor Life.
- **Open issues box:** "Baby due in March."

## Tone
Funny. Dry and relatable, like one rep laughing at the thing every new rep dreads. The stick figures keep it light.

- **Voices:** one AI voice for the stick figure, one for the coworker, one calm narrator.
- **Fake only:** Jane Doe and all facts are made up. No real people and no real client data.
- **Not a pitch:** it explains a problem in my field and shows the app doing something about it.
