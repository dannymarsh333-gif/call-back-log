# Ad script

## Length
30 seconds.

## The scenes
| Seconds | On screen | Voice or text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | THE HOOK. Close-up of a young rep at a desk, phone to his ear, eyes wide, frozen mid-call. A friendly voice is heard from the phone, but he is staring at a blank notepad. | Rep (thinking, whispered): "Was it her husband... or her brother who lost the job?" | Office hum. A single awkward silence, then a clock ticking. |
| 5–15 | THE PROBLEM MOMENT. Quick cuts: a desk covered in sticky notes, three spreadsheet tabs, a phone notes app with a mess of text, a calendar with a missed reminder. Back to the rep, sweating. The voice on the phone says "Hello? Still there?" | Narrator (dry, friendly): "Dozens of calls. Scraps of notes. And a callback you forgot two days ago." On-screen text: "Who was this again?" | Paper rustling, fast clicking, the ticking speeds up. A sad trombone-style note on "forgot." |
| 15–25 | THE APP SOLVING IT. Real screen recording of Call Back Log on a phone (sample contacts only). Today's callbacks list with one red "2 days overdue" card. Tap the card. The "Open issues" box shows "Husband recently lost his job." Cut to the rep after a call, saying his notes into the phone. | Narrator: "Call Back Log. Tap a name, see the last conversation. After the call, just say what happened." | Music comes in: light, upbeat. A soft "ding" when the card opens. |
| 25–30 | THE CLOSING LINE. The rep, relaxed, smiling, on a call with his feet up. Final title card on a calm blue background with the app name. | Rep (warmly): "Maria! How is your husband's job search going?" Title card text: "Call Back Log. Remember every call." | Music resolves with a short button. One last "ding." Silence. |

## Tone
Funny. Dry and relatable, like one rep laughing at the thing every new rep dreads. Never mocking the people on the phone.

## Notes for November 19
- **Use the real app for the app scene.** Record the actual Call Back Log screen. AI video tools can't draw accurate app screens, so don't ask them to generate the UI.
- **Use AI tools for the human scenes:** the rep, the messy desk, and the smiling finish. Each shot is 3 to 5 seconds, so generate them one at a time and trim.
- **Voice:** an AI voice for the narrator (calm, friendly) and a second voice for the rep.
- **Fake only.** Sample names such as "Maria Lopez" and no real client data or real people's faces.
- **Not a pitch.** The ad explains a problem in my field and shows the app doing something about it. Nothing is being sold.
- **Check the length.** 30 seconds is about 70 spoken words, and this script is under that.
