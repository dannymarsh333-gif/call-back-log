## Headline
Never walk into a follow-up call cold again.

## Subhead
New reps make dozens of calls a week, and the details that matter slip away between them. Call Back Log keeps what each person told you, and tells you who to call today.

## How the app helps
1. After each call, it asks three quick questions (topics, companies, next steps) and lets you answer by talking.
2. Every contact keeps a timeline and a list of open life issues, so you can see the last conversation in seconds before you dial.
3. Your home screen shows today's callbacks, and anything you missed rolls over in yellow and then red so no one is forgotten.

## Look and feel
- Colors: calm blue as the main color, white cards on a light grey background. Yellow and red only for overdue callbacks, always with a text label such as "2 days overdue."
- Tone: plain, friendly, like one rep talking to another. No sales language, no jargon.
- Image: one phone-sized screenshot of the Today's callbacks screen with the sample contacts (fake names only). No stock photos.
- Mobile first. Large tap targets, readable in a quick glance between calls.
